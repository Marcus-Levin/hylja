### How to read the `Dependencies` column

* It reproduces the issue's **own** `## Dependencies` list from its frozen public issue body — in that body's **own bullet order**, which is why the cells are not sorted. It is **not** a campaign-relevance subset, and it is **not** a campaign ordering. Campaign reasoning lives entirely in the `Evidence gate` column, so no cell can be misread as the issue's declared dependency set.
* How a cell is derived: only the issue **of each bullet** in the frozen `## Dependencies` list counts. An id appearing only inside a bullet's trailing annotation prose — for example `#13`'s `#68 — semantic-preserving transformation requirements informed by #43 evidence` — is **not** a declared dependency and must not appear in a cell.
* Coverage of the frozen snapshot is `#6`–`#8`, `#11`–`#39` and `#65`–`#68`. **`#9` and `#10` are not in the frozen public snapshot**, and `#65`–`#68` declare **no** `## Dependencies` section. Those cells say so explicitly rather than carrying an inferred list; the campaign relationship for `#65`–`#68` comes from [#38](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/38)'s own frozen sequencing text, quoted by attribution in the *Dependency order* section below.
