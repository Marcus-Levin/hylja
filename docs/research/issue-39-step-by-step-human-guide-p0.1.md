# How to run Hylja's small synthetic privacy comparison — a guide for newcomers

**PUBLIC GUIDE, NOT APPROVAL.** You do not need to know Hylja: it is an early project exploring whether an AI-assisted engineering task can stay useful without sending information to a destination that should not receive it. The proposed [#39 benchmark](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/39) is a **made-up, local comparison**, not a deployed privacy safeguard. These instructions describe the order in which *people must make and check decisions*; they do not appoint anyone, create an answer key, approve a policy, freeze a benchmark or authorize a scored run. For implementation status, use [the plan](../plan.md#current-state), not this guide.

## Read this first: what are we testing?

- A **candidate** is a program being evaluated. The current public reference is a practice candidate: it has already seen public examples and was built *before* the required advance agreement on rules. It **cannot** be passed off as an untouched scored baseline.
- An **oracle** is the independently written expected-answer key: which made-up details are planted, what kind they are, what may reach a particular destination and what a correct engineering answer requires. A candidate's own output must never write or repair the oracle.
- The **destination** or **sink** is the specific model/recipient a test send would reach; a storage location for hidden tests is **not** that destination. `KEEP` means leave a value visible, `MASK` means hide it irreversibly, and `BLOCK` means stop the attempted send. These are questions for a human policy reviewer at a *verified test destination*, never permission to send real data.
- **Task fidelity** means preserving the detail needed for a correct engineering answer: for example, the relationship between escalation contacts, a diagnostic port mismatch, or valid JSON. It never overrules a privacy denial.
- **Public practice cases** D01, D02 and D05 are visible to candidate developers. H01–H04 are *planning names*, not existing hidden cases. A **blind** case is a future synthetic sealed exam question and answer kept from everyone building or tuning candidates.

**Never put real customer/employee/infrastructure/credential data, reviewer identities, hidden test contents or answers, seeds, keys, restricted locations or sensitive content-derived hashes in Git, pull requests, CI, ordinary chat or a candidate-accessible workspace.** Ask the project sponsor for an approved private way to handle identities, approvals and custody records. A second worktree under the same computer account is *not* separation. An AI agent can help draft and check public material; it cannot be an independent human approver or blind custodian.

## Steps in order — each numbered step has a clear stop point

### 1. Name the coordinator and ask people about roles (can start now)

**Who:** sponsor/protocol steward and evaluation owner. **Do:** privately identify who will coordinate the procedure and who owns evaluation execution. Invite separate people to discuss (a) information-category research #65, (b) privacy/trust/task-detail design #66, (c) destination-specific policy, (d) engineering task correctness, (e) scoring, and (f) independent blind-case authorship/custody. A *second independent human* must check hidden answers and custody controls, and someone independent checks lock chronology. Existing volunteers may discuss more than one role, but disclose candidate-development/public-result exposure, any blind access and role overlaps; arrange an independent conflict check. The coordinator cannot approve everybody's work alone.

**Done when:** a **private role record** says for each appointment who accepted, competence, conflicts, permitted access, who independently checks it and the date. A public “I'm willing” message is *not* an appointment or sign-off. Use the short [policy](issue-39-policy-reviewer-handoff-p0.1.md), [task](issue-39-engineering-reviewer-handoff-p0.1.md), [scoring](issue-39-scoring-reviewer-handoff-p0.1.md), [custodian](issue-39-blind-custodian-handoff-p0.1.md) invitations and [research-role worksheet](issue-39-65-66-prefreeze-worksheet-p0.1.md) to begin safely. **Stop** if an independent reviewer or private channel cannot be arranged.

### 2. Research the information types and task-detail rules (no lock yet)

**Who:** appointed #65 research lead and different human reviewer; appointed #66 design lead and different human reviewer. **Do:** #65 examines personal data including Swedish national identifiers, engineering content versus identifiers, ambiguities, existing classifier compatibility and explicit gaps. The [source index](issue-65-public-source-index-p0.1.md) is a starting list of limited citations, **not** the answer. #66 distinguishes what a value *is*, whether privacy rules might apply, disclosure sensitivity, whether its source may influence a decision, and what task details must survive; the [design discussion seed](issue-66-dimensions-fidelity-seed-p0.1.md) is questions, **not** a contract. Neither research lead chooses which actual destination may receive a value.

**Done when:** each research result has a version, sources, definitions, uncertainty/unsupported areas, compatibility/migration implications and a dated independent human review. The two reviewers state what vocabulary is solid enough *to propose* for #39, not that their study automatically approves the benchmark or closes all broader work. **Stop** before any v0 freeze if the oracle fields or task-fidelity vocabulary still depend on unanswered #65/#66 questions.

### 3. Choose a truly separate way to hold future blind material (planning may run alongside step 2)

**Who:** proposed custodian plus a separate, qualified human who reviews both storage controls and future hidden answers. **Do:** evaluate an audited private cloud store *or* controlled offline encrypted media; either may be rejected. Candidate developers and CI must lack direct **and inherited administrator/key** access. Check separate identities/keys, access and key-use records, independent log review, backups, retention, revocation and isolated scoring. Offline media additionally needs independently reconciled custody/transfer logs; a private repository, encryption checkbox, handwritten log alone, or same-user worktree is insufficient.

**Done when:** a restricted, dated independent control review approves a specific arrangement and who may access what. Publicly share only a reviewed, privacy-safe attestation that controls were checked, **not** the store address, key, roster or hidden-derived digest. **Stop:** do not create, upload or hand over even one blind case until this review passes and step 2's needed vocabulary is ready.

### 4. Agree what the *small* comparison will and will not cover

**Who:** protocol steward and evaluation owner, with an independent scope review. **Do:** the sponsor's [public direction](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/39#issuecomment-5843225618) favors a deliberately narrow D01/D02/D05 *proposal* and a strict pre-tuning lock; it does **not** approve the supported matrix. Determine whether those public practice families plus **planned future** structurally distinct, executable blind families can be supported at a controlled synthetic text/JSON `model.input` sink; the blind cases are only authored in step 6. If not supportable, stop and return to the sponsor rather than silently claiming full #39 coverage. Write a table of supported **family × surface × representation × information class**. Label all other areas **unsupported**, **not tested**, or **outside this architecture**; none is a pass. The broad #39 workload list and H01–H04 planning names are not automatic executable coverage.

**Done when:** the scope and exclusions have an approved version and independent review, referencing accepted #65/#66-relevant findings. A narrow evidence gate does **not** automatically close #39 or prove production interception.

### 5. Obtain *three separate* decisions on policy, correct tasks and scoring

**Who:** an independent public-oracle author and human reviewer prepare the draft answer key; distinct policy, engineering-task and scoring humans respectively approve their own parts. None authors from candidate output. **Do:** using the approved scope and research vocabulary, the policy reviewer independently defines destination-specific treatment **rules and public planted-occurrence expectations for a specified controlled test sink/profile**, conditional on a future runner actually verifying that route; include missing/unknown-route fallback. D02's made-up token is a real open choice between irreversible `MASK` and terminal `BLOCK`—the public proposal and exposed reference disagree, and neither wins by default. The task reviewer independently writes **public** correct outcomes and required-detail criteria: D01 contact escalation ordering, D02 HTTPS attempted 443 versus expected 8443, D05 valid JSON/HTTPS port/path/Linux output-file behavior based on what a candidate may see. The scoring reviewer separately fixes matching, denominators, `n/a`, false hiding, task success, attempted versus *delivered* leaks, body **and** metadata, hard failures, weights or unweighted comparison, and `untested`/blocked cases **before** seeing any eligible scored result. For matching, they must define how a planted source-field position maps through field identity, normalization, line joins and JSON-leaf projection to the candidate's reported UTF-8 byte positions, including overlaps/repeats; two different position conventions cannot simply be compared as numbers. Hidden-occurrence treatments and hidden task answers cannot be approved until cases are authored in step 6; their restricted independent review must precede the lock.

**Done when:** each separate human signs a versioned restricted record of decision, rationale, objections, conflicts, date and independently checked scope; an independently written public oracle/rubric revision reflects approved, non-hidden conclusions. No AI or coordinator can sign for them. **Stop** if policy treatment is selected to favor the reference or if a blocked/unobserved send is called a solved task or a privacy pass.

### 6. Author and independently check sealed synthetic cases

**Who:** authorized blind author/custodian and a separate human answer-key/custody reviewer, with restricted independent policy/task checks of blind truth. **Do:** only after steps 2–5 and the approved storage controls, create future cases *inside the restricted store*, not rewrites of D01/D02/D05. Include safe positive cases, harmless details that should stay (negative controls), denied/blocked attempts, and task outcomes as the supported scope permits. The independent blind reviewer checks planted answer-key independence from candidate output, structural independence from public families, repeated values, encoded/partial leaks, metadata and task correctness. Give qualified policy and task reviewers **restricted, logged access** to check each hidden occurrence's treatment and each hidden task answer against the approved rules/criteria; anyone who sees blind contents must not develop, tune or direct the candidate. Keep labels, templates, answers, seeds, restricted hashes and access records from candidate developers and CI.

**Done when:** the independent blind reviewer and separate qualified policy/task reviewers record restricted approvals of exact hidden answers/treatments, custody and supported-scope fit; only privacy-safe opaque references may leave the store. **Stop** on any candidate-developer or unapproved account access; re-evaluate eligibility and create a new held-out version rather than quietly reusing exposed answers.

### 7. Make the **prospective lock before building a fresh eligible candidate**

**Who:** protocol steward, evaluation owner, all owners of their distinct decisions, and an independent chronology reviewer. **Do:** *before* an eligible candidate is built, the evaluation owner must independently demonstrate a **minimal executable #5 seam** using public synthetic cases and an independent planted-oracle proposal: a practice-only callback, at least one graded engineering task, proposed destination treatment/denial, and evaluator-owned capture of the exact locally serialized body **and** metadata. Check the declared representation; no capture alone authenticates an actual route or proves enforcement. Then version and integrity-record the approved scope/exclusions, public versus blind family split, independently reviewed oracle/answers, destination policy/treatment, task prompts/fidelity, rubric/scoring and critical gates, candidate input contract and runner requirements. The custodian holds any restricted corpus and sensitive digest; public material may contain only independently approved non-sensitive hashes and privacy-safe attestations. Verify the dated lock came **before** candidate development/tuning. If an approved answer or rule changes, make a *new version* and independently held-out cases; never revise truth to fit a candidate.

**Done when:** an independent human verifies the minimal public seam's executable evidence **and** the lock's version identities, distinct approvals and chronology. The already exposed reference remains *exploratory practice only*; its prior use cannot be retroactively made pre-tuning locked. **Stop** if the declared scope cannot be exercised by that seam or the prospective record cannot be proved; no fresh candidate is eligible yet.

### 8. Build the new candidate, then freeze the *exact runnable test* before any blind score

**Who:** candidate developer (public materials only), evaluation owner and independent runner/custody reviewer. **Do:** develop a **fresh** candidate after step 7 without accessing blind cases or answers. Keep public development diagnostics unscored. **Complete and harden** the already demonstrated minimal seam into a separately approved isolated scored runner that independently binds the actual controlled sink/profile, captures the **final serialized body and metadata**, verifies zero-byte blocks, prevents uncontrolled file/network output, and keeps answer keys away from the candidate. Today's local #5 capture/callback is useful practice but **does not authenticate routing, isolate execution, intercept external traffic or enforce policy**. Use *only public or separately designated rehearsal-only synthetic controls* while validating this runner; **neither candidate developers nor runner-validation staff may preview or execute eligible blind cases or their outputs before the step-9 approved blind run**. The restricted human answer-key checks in step 6 are separate and never reach developers. Pin exact versions/hashes of candidate, synthetic inputs, restricted oracle, policy, scorer, runner and configuration in a **separate final freeze before blind execution**; restricted hashes stay restricted. A changed runner, rule or answer needs a reviewed new version before scoring.

**Implementer check:** the [public development oracle](issue-39-public-development-oracle-p0.1.json) defines a `sourceSpan` as zero-based, half-open Unicode **code-point** positions inside a *decoded source-field string* (`source.path`) **before** normalization or line joining—not positions in the fixture's serialized JSON and not the candidate's UTF-8 byte offsets. Verify field identity and any normalization, LF join or JSON-leaf projection before matching spans, including repeats and overlaps. The bounded D05 ASCII/unescaped projector is **not** a general blind-case mapper. If the declared cases cannot be mapped unambiguously, stop their scored use instead of guessing.

**Done when:** independent public/rehearsal-only tests/checks cover positive/harmless, denial, blocked-send, leakage and engineering utility on the declared representations, and the isolated runner plus final freeze pass human review. **Stop** if a route is unverified or a possible send cannot be reliably observed; this is `untested`, not safe release.

### 9. Run and report the *non-enforcing* synthetic baseline

**Who:** isolated authorized runner/scorer; independent policy/task/scoring and custody reviewers verify their own evidence. **Do:** run only supported synthetic cases and report unscored public practice separately from eligible held-out blind results; compare independently planted truth with detection and the bytes actually delivered to the controlled destination. Count misses even if a later check stopped a leak; distinguish attempted from delivered leakage. An observed zero-byte `BLOCK` avoids *that* send but leaves the engineering task unsolved. A missing or unreliable capture is **untested**. Publish applicability, unsupported areas, safe counters/denominators/`n/a`, task outcomes, hard-gate outcomes, exact public version references and limitations—never raw planted values, hidden answers, sensitive digests or ordinary model traffic. Custodian retains restricted evidence and private approval records for independent replay.

**Done when:** humans independently sign a privacy-safe report tied to the exact frozen version. This remains a **bounded, NON-ENFORCING synthetic comparison**, not production safety proof; do not score #40–#47 candidates or choose a #48 component here.

## A one-minute sponsor checklist

1. **Today:** ask for an approved private channel; invite/qualify human #65/#66 leads and independent reviewers; check the separate hidden-answer/custody reviewer; ask custodians which independently auditable storage choice they can operate. Record *willingness* separately from appointments.
2. **Next:** obtain reviewed #65/#66 findings and a storage audit; only then ask the protocol/evaluation owners and separate policy/task/scoring humans for exact versioned decisions. Do not create hidden cases before the storage review.
3. **Later:** sealed cases + independently proved minimal public #5 seam → prospective lock → fresh candidate → final executable freeze → isolated synthetic blind run → privacy-safe independently reviewed report. If any arrow is skipped, **stop** and ask for a corrected version, not an exception invented by an AI assistant.

The [#39 open-gates packet](issue-39-v0-open-gates-p0.1.md) records detailed pending questions; [the evaluation policy](../evaluation.md) explains why detection, final-byte leakage and task utility are different evidence layers. This guide itself does not mark any gate complete.
