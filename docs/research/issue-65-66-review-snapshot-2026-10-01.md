## Proposed findings to challenge

| Finding | Proposed direction already discussed | Specific boundary for independent review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T1: entities and identifiers** | `PERSON` denotes a natural-person entity. Neutral `IDENTIFIER` contains contact, national, online and financial identifiers; personal-data status is a separate contextual attribute. The pinned draft is wider than those four: its `IDENTIFIER` type uses five domains — `CONTACT`, `NATIONAL_ID`, `ONLINE`, `EMPLOYMENT` and `FINANCIAL` — with `EMPLOYEE_NUMBER` recorded under `EMPLOYMENT` as tenant-specific formats, while `IDENTITY`, `PERSONAL_PROFILE`, `AUTHENTICATION` and `IT` are domains of other types. | A shared mailbox and a corporate bank account must not become a person merely because of their type. Decide whether contact points need their own type, whether `EMPLOYMENT`/`EMPLOYEE_NUMBER` really belongs to a neutral identifier family, whether the domain list is right, and specify overlap precedence. |
| **T2: engineering content** | Keep `ENGINEERING_IDENTIFIER` for tags/numbers; use `ENGINEERING_INFORMATION` with domains for content such as a BOM, topology or setpoint. Tenant-specific recognition requires scoped evidence. | Source-check each included subtype. Do not infer recognition or parser support from a standards title. Explicitly defer the existing CAE, IM, IT and other source gaps where relevant. |
| **M1: independent dimensions** | Semantic identity, contextual privacy attributes, sensitivity, source influence, task fidelity and treatment have separate homes. Privacy attributes use `YES`/`NO`/`UNKNOWN`; a semantic `NO` alone cannot clear a concern. | Check conflicting claims and who authenticates trusted context. An IP may carry contextual personal-data concern while remaining semantically an IP; these attributes are not legal determinations. |
| **M2: conservative disagreement** | Keep a sound deterministic sensitivity floor. Semantic judgment may raise concern; disagreement is retained as `RESOLVED_CONSERVATIVELY`. Explicit semantic abstention is an absent opinion; any failure or uncertain floor remains unresolved and held. | Review the abstention/failure distinction, parser/configured-source evidence, escalation metering and policy handling.
