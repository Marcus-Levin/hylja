## 1. Method, budgets and how to read every line below

* **Two axes, kept separate.** Provenance is `claim/docs` for a vendor page or document read here and `source-inspected` for public API/registry metadata. **No `independently-executed` or `vendor-demonstrated` evidence exists anywhere in this file**, so the observed outcome is **empty (no observed outcome)** for every capability named below. A published price, tier or roadmap entry is not a measured capability, and no price proves any capability.
* **`UNKNOWN` means evidence was not established**, not that a feature, price or permission is absent. An inaccessible page is `UNKNOWN`/`BLOCKED` for that request and is **not** proof that no public price exists.
* **Quote-only status requires an inspected statement.** It is never inferred from the absence of a number, and no rounded, range or projected figure is given for any plan.
* **Per-source quotation budget.** Verbatim wording stays under 25 words for any single page, and material attributed to a single page stays inside a 200-word allowance. The count is reproducible: a **claim unit** is a bullet, one prose cell of a table row, one source-list line, or one sentence of a prose paragraph; **every word of a unit is charged to the pages cited in that unit, split evenly when it cites several** — including an uncited bold lead-in or project-local note inside that same unit, and including the §2 ledger description cells and §9 source-list labels; uncited project-local sentences in a prose paragraph (status, gates, checks) are not vendor-derived and are not charged. Measured over the 40 vendor pages charged by that rule across this record and the register, dossier and plan pointers at this head, the worst page carries **187.4 attributed words and 20 verbatim words**. Vendor wording is therefore **paraphrased by default**, with only a few short load-bearing fragments quoted; page titles, plan names, plan amounts, defined-term names and URLs are facts and are not treated as reproduced prose. Every quotation below was re-verified against the retrieved page.
* **Product/tier applicability is stated each time.** Platform-wide terms, one product line, one plan and one browser extension are different scopes, and no statement is transferred between them.
* **No** customer name, employee, address, credential or vendor-side identifier from any page is reproduced here.
