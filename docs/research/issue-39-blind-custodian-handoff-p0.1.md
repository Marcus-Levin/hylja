# Invitation: independent holder of future hidden synthetic test cases

**PUBLIC INVITATION ONLY — not a custody appointment, access grant, approved test plan or score.** You do not need to know Hylja. It is an early research project testing whether a privacy layer could hide private details before they reach an AI model used by an engineering assistant while leaving useful task information intact. Its current examples are made up and run only in a local test setup; there is no deployed privacy safeguard.

## Why we need a separate person

The *reference candidate* is the experimental program being tested. Its developers may see public practice cases, but not future *blind* cases and answers—the sealed exam. H01–H04 name possible case categories; they are **not existing cases**. Anyone who accesses hidden contents or answers must not develop, tune or direct the candidate while those cases remain eligible. An AI assistant cannot be your independent human reviewer or custodian. The public [policy](issue-39-policy-reviewer-handoff-p0.1.md) and [engineering-task](issue-39-engineering-reviewer-handoff-p0.1.md) invitations seek two different roles. **Those two volunteers plus you do not fill the whole review team:** a separately designated scoring reviewer and test-procedure owner must approve their own parts, and a second human must review hidden answers and custody controls. Replying to an invitation appoints nobody.

## Which store might you operate? Neither yet is fine

Neither option is approved just because it is encrypted. A future store must keep hidden data, labels, seeds, keys and sensitive integrity digests **outside Git, pull requests, automated CI builds, chat and candidate-accessible workspaces**. Candidate developers and CI must lack direct *and inherited administrator/key* access. Independently review who can read/write, how every access is accounted for, retention, backup/copies and revocation. Another worktree under the same operating-system user is **not** separation.

- **Private cloud store:** a dedicated encrypted vault/bucket, separately administered encryption key, least-privilege custodian/evaluator accounts, and independently reviewable protected logs for storage reads/writes *and key use*. Deny candidate/CI access to both the store and its key, including inherited admin roles; review retention and revocation. A private repository or encryption checkbox alone is not an audit.
- **Offline encrypted drive:** physical custody of encrypted removable media and any backups, separate key custody, no mounting on a candidate developer's machine, and an isolated scorer. Keep a dated, numbered, signed record of every access and media transfer, stored separately and regularly reconciled by another human against physical media/key inventory. A handwritten log alone cannot prove every access or make this option automatically eligible; if its controls cannot be independently checked, defer blind work.

In a future approved run, an isolated candidate may receive a synthetic test input, but **never the answer key**; only an authorized scorer compares its captured final output with the hidden answer afterward. Block uncontrolled network or file output. The existing local test callback and captured bytes are **not** an isolation boundary. Keep private access records and content-derived digests with the custodian; only a reviewed privacy-safe attestation or opaque reference may appear publicly. This invitation creates no blind score or final protocol freeze.
