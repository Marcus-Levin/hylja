`UNKNOWN` means evidence was not established, not that a feature or risk is absent. Dates and release labels are the source's published metadata, not a tested binary identity. A release tag or model revision is a *desk snapshot*; it is not a pin of the eventual wheel, image, binary or weight file. The table mixes **trial gates** (identity/origin, exact artifact and evaluation rights, available hash/provenance comparison, readily available critical advisories) with **adoption research gaps** (broader dependencies, SBOM, maintenance, commercial split and replaceability). An adoption gap alone does not bar an isolated synthetic trial. The issue numbers show which comparison would use each candidate.

## Advisory and provenance check performed

On 2026-09-28, the public GitHub repository security-advisory endpoints were queried for the listed public repositories. Presidio, Privacy Filter, both CloakPipe identities, Kiji, Jev CLI, Pleno and MCP Conceal returned no *repository-published* advisories; LiteLLM returned published advisories including the historical critical item above. OSV's public version query returned no direct records for PyPI `presidio-analyzer==2.2.364`, `presidio-anonymizer==2.2.364`, `litellm==1.103.0` and `pleno-presidio-extras==0.1.1`. These narrow negative results **do not clear** dependencies, images, model files, undisclosed issues, alternate ecosystems or commercial binaries. The queries can be replayed with [GitHub's advisory API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/security-advisories/repository-advisories#list-repository-security-advisories) and [OSV's version-query API](https://google.github.io/osv.dev/api/#tag/OSV/operation/OSV_QueryAffected).

## Execution gate and follow-on requirements

The [2026-10-01 Presidio artifact inspection](issue-46-presidio-artifact-prescreen-2026-10-01.md) follows up the Presidio row at the wheel level: exact analyzer/anonymizer download origins and byte-verified SHA-256 values, actual wheel metadata, absent published PGP signatures, sdists left unselected, missing packaged license notices, published attestations whose certificate contents were parsed but not cryptographically verified, and refreshed direct advisory queries. That records the origin, license, digest-comparison and direct-advisory items of the Presidio trial gate **for those two files only**. It does **not** close the gate's "signatures or provenance where available" item: the attestations are still unverified, the certificate-claimed source commit is the release tag commit's parent rather than the tag commit, and no reproducible-build or authenticated-publisher conclusion was reached. It does not clear dependencies, NLP weights or recognizer configuration, execute the candidate, supply observed quality outcomes or approve adoption; the redistribution-notice gap and the dated v0.1 desk snapshot above both remain open.

**Current evidence: no third-party execution has been cleared by v0.1.** Before a *selected* binary, package or weight is run, record canonical source/owner, exact artifact/version/download origin, its license and permitted evaluation use; compare published hashes, signatures or provenance where available and record their absence otherwise; check readily available critical advisories for that artifact/version. Pin it and use only an isolated disposable environment, synthetic fixtures, disposable credentials and controlled sinks. For a hosted synthetic Jev arm, establish the account/tier and evaluation terms and keep the controlled external boundary explicit. Resolve CloakPipe's license discrepancy and any ambiguous commercial trial rights for the chosen artifact. **Full** dependency/SBOM, maintenance, provenance, legal/commercial and exit review is the later **adoption** gate under #46, not a precondition to desk research or an otherwise prescreened synthetic trial. This document neither authorizes traffic nor freezes #39's scored protocol.

**2026-10-01 follow-up (Presidio row).** The [provenance and runtime prescreen follow-up](issue-46-presidio-provenance-runtime-prescreen-2026-10-01.md) closes the attestation item for the two selected wheels only: both PEP 740 bundles were verified check by check against a digest-pinned, independently corroborated trust anchor, and the certificate's identity was compared against independently fetched GitHub records rather than accepted as stated. It does **not** close the gate: the published attestation is the publish predicate only, so no build or reproducible-build evidence exists, the sdists remain unselected, and the wheels still package no license notice. It additionally pins and screens the anonymizer's minimum runtime and reports one unscored, synthetic-only `mask`/`hash` smoke inside an isolated ephemeral sandbox; that smoke is development evidence, not an observed #38 outcome. The analyzer, its eight mandatory dependencies, every NLP configuration, model weight and hosted-service extra remain unselected and unscreened, and `cryptography` 48.0.1 — the only in-range version `presidio-anonymizer` allows — carries two HIGH and one MODERATE advisory with no in-range fix, which is an open adoption blocker candidate.

Concrete [#33](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/33) requirements to consider: a lockable multi-artifact manifest (including detector weights, NLP language packs, proxy image, guardrail and SDK); license/tier attestations tied to each digest; authenticated publisher provenance and reproducible or independently verified hashes where offered; an SBOM plus dependency and advisory review at the pinned version; a maintained security-disclosure and patch path; a documented replacement/exit path for every detector, proxy, vault and hosted judge. None is yet evidenced for an integrated candidate stack. For #48, any role needing these unproved controls remains **deferred**, rather than scoring source claims as a pass.
