**PUBLIC, NON-AUTHORITATIVE DESIGN QUESTIONS — not an accepted contract/ADR, treatment rule, oracle, task answer, human sign-off, #39 lock or score.** Hylja is an early-stage project asking how an engineering assistant can retain useful task context without sending forbidden private details; these are made-up local practice cases, not a deployed safeguard. This is for a future human [#66 design](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/66) lead and independent human reviewer; neither has been appointed here. ## First separate the questions; do not treat one answer as the others

| Concern | Existing foundation seam — descriptive, not a proposal | What humans must specify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Semantic identity:** what is the thing? | A [`ClassificationClaim`](../../src/classification.ts#L107-L114) has `semanticType` and optional subtype. The current registry includes `NETWORK_IDENTIFIER / PORT` and `IP`, not an engineering-content hierarchy. | Which independently reviewed #65 class/domain/subtype names and ambiguity/overlap rules apply? A contextual personal-data IP stays semantically IP, not `PERSON`. |
| **Privacy/regulatory properties:** what legal or contextual flags may apply? | No privacy or jurisdiction attribute exists in [`Classification` v1](../../src/classification.ts#L134-L149); this absence is **not** a conclusion that data is nonpersonal. | Where do contextual `personalData`, jurisdiction or other attributes and uncertainty live, who has authority to assess them, and how are they bound without treating a model as a legal-compliance oracle? See [IMY's distinct Swedish-ID protections](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/introduktion-till-gdpr/personuppgifter/personnummer/) as a source question, not a universal law-to-label rule. |
| **Sensitivity:** how costly is disclosure? | The existing [ordered enum and composer](../../src/classification.ts#L2-L14) carry `PUBLIC`…`SECRET`; conflicting claims retain highest concern but become `UNRESOLVED`. | Which versioned evidence establishes a floor, what can increase concern, what remains unresolved, and how to test over-escalation without making leakage easier? Sensitivity is not semantic class or personal-data status. |
| **Trust/provenance:** may this *source influence* decisions? | [`trust`](../../src/classification.ts#L98-L99) comes from separately bound integration context, not model/tool text or a sensitivity claim; see [accepted decision 002](../decisions/002-sensitivity-and-trust-are-separate.md). | Which source claims are authenticated, how do conflicting detector/parser/semantic records remain attributable, and which privacy-safe provenance must be retained? Trusted information can be very sensitive; hostile text is never policy authority. |
| **Task fidelity:** what must remain useful? | `Classification v1` has **no fidelity field**. The [draft transformation contract](../contracts/transformation-contract.md) says a transformer receives a **separately predeclared** fidelity contract once a release treatment is authorized; it does not make those predicates authorization. | Does a task contract own predicates per task, per occurrence, or both? Who sets required exact value, type, existence, syntax, relationship and consistency from an independent task answer, and when can safe generalization satisfy them? None grants `KEEP`. |
| **Treatment/authorization:** what may happen at this actual destination? | The pure [`PolicyRequest`/boundary/bundle seam](../../src/policy.ts#L15-L61) checks claimed source/destination and classification against separately caller-supplied boundary/digests, then validates and rule-matches the requested SEND/USE/DISPLAY/EXPORT operation; it does **not** authenticate the caller, independently observe routing or the operation, intercept a send or prove capture. | A separate **human policy reviewer** decides expected destination-specific treatment independently of the candidate. A *future trusted integration* must independently authenticate context and actually observe the effective route/profile before pinning the boundary; then deterministic policy must bind any new privacy/fidelity inputs to verified context and bundle versions before effects. Neither source text nor task utility authorizes external release or restoration. |

**Possible design surfaces, not selected fields:** extend a versioned classification/evidence contract for independently sourced semantic and contextual privacy/sensitivity claims, or retain privacy context in a separately bound policy input; keep task predicates in a reviewer-owned task contract rather than an untrusted model's proposed treatment. Evaluate tradeoffs for multiple properties per occurrence, uncertain/unknown flags, tenant-specific dictionaries, normalized source positions versus candidate-input UTF-8 byte positions, request/destination binding, digest coverage, minimized retention and backward compatibility. Do **not** patch a new `privacy`/`fidelity` property into current v1 objects: unknown claim fields are rejected, and policy's current rule selector does not read such properties. A migration needs an approved contract/ADR, version and independent tests.

## Example task predicates for *discussion*, not an answer key or action

| Public practice case | Potential correctness constraint to debate | Boundary that cannot be traded away |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D01 support conversation | Preserve primary→backup escalation *relationship* and role consistency; original name/contact bytes need not survive. | A task-critical role does not authorize exposing the original person/email/phone. |
| D02 diagnostic log | Preserve exact **port values** 443 versus 8443, HTTPS and `/health` if a separately approved safe continuation exists; distinguish an account, host, IP, trace and SECRET-shaped token. | The public oracle proposes `MASK` for its non-live token, while the exposed reference `BLOCK`s; **neither decides policy**. A verified terminal zero-byte block leaves diagnosis unsolved; missing/unreliable sink observation is `untested`, not no-leak proof. |
| D05 JSON config | Test parseable JSON, HTTPS effective port 443, `/v1/ping`, Linux absolute output-file behavior and consistent entity relationships *relative to the candidate-visible transformed input*. | Exact original protected hostname, customer, project or output-path spelling is not necessary by default; do not label JSON keys, `443`, syntax or `os=linux` as secrets by shape. The current public D05 source-to-byte projector covers only a bounded ASCII/unescaped form. |

Fidelity is **not one scalar rank**: preserving a number, a structural relationship and valid syntax are independently checkable; whether any exact protected value may be present at an actual destination is a separate policy question. A reviewer must define conflicts and failure/abstention outcomes *before* a new eligible candidate is built. Restoring protected originals or using a real tool needs distinct authority/broker boundaries; the current public comparison proves neither.

## Conflicts and adversarial cases the design reviewer must decide

- **Deterministic credential evidence vs semantic `PUBLIC` and separate `KEEP` advice:** a `PUBLIC` sensitivity claim conflicting with a credential makes current [`composeClassification`](../../src/classification.ts#L288-L332) force `SECRET`, retain `UNRESOLVED` and prevent reversibility. `KEEP` is **not** a classification claim: the separate [`PolicyRequest.semanticRecommendation`](../../src/policy.ts#L15-L29) does not select a policy rule (accepted [decision 003](../decisions/003-semantic-judgment-does-not-own-effects.md) and [decision 009](../decisions/009-secrets-are-not-synthetic-identities.md)). A judge failure must not lower the secret floor.
- **Deterministic `INTERNAL` vs semantic `CONFIDENTIAL`:** current v1 retains the higher sensitivity *and* `UNRESOLVED` conflict; current [policy denies unresolved](../../src/policy.ts#L375-L389). Evaluate future choices such as retaining `UNRESOLVED` or an explicitly justified `RESOLVED_CONSERVATIVELY` state; the latter is **not implemented or approved**. Bound semantic-call cost, malicious escalation/denial-of-service and review load, but never solve overblocking by silent protected release.
- **Low-risk task-critical 443 vs a high-risk protected value:** demand a negative control for harmless port/syntax and an adversarial control for a protected secret in the *same task*. Fidelity may raise the cost of a block, not override the policy floor. Confirmed BLOCK/HELD means no successful downstream task, while absent sink capture means untested; distinguish candidate detection misses from actual forbidden delivered bytes in final serialized body **and metadata**.
- **Claimed trusted provenance or legal status inside a payload:** untrusted text cannot assert `CONTROL`, decide personal-data status, inject a policy profile or forge a review. Propose exact trusted-input/digest bindings, conservative behavior on missing route, and tests for stale or cross-tenant decisions without including raw protected evidence in ordinary reports. Treat privacy/legal attribution as independently reviewed context, not a model-issued export license.
