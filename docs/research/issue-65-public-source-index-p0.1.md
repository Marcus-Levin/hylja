## How to use these sources

Read a link for **only the claim it supports**, then have an independent qualified human check the source, definitions, version and jurisdiction. A source's domain vocabulary does **not** itself determine confidentiality, personal-data status for every value, destination treatment or whether an engineering answer remains correct. PLM/PDM means product-lifecycle/product-data management; CAD/CAE/CAM mean computer-aided design/engineering/manufacturing; PMI means product/manufacturing information; OT means operational technology; IM means information management; NC means numerical control. No proprietary standard text is reproduced here. Public standards descriptions may only establish that a subject deserves investigation; where a full standard is licensed, review it through a legitimate route rather than treating its title as a complete taxonomy.

## Comparison with current code, not a compatibility promise

In current [`classification.ts`](../../src/classification.ts), `SEMANTIC_CLASSES` include `PERSON`, `NETWORK_IDENTIFIER`, `HOST_OR_SERVICE`, `ENGINEERING_IDENTIFIER` and `BUSINESS_CONFIDENTIAL`; the existing `ENGINEERING_IDENTIFIER` default subtypes cover asset, drawing, part, item, document, functional-location, PLC and SCADA **identifiers/tags**, not non-ID engineering content. `PERSON` currently has `NAME`/`EMAIL`/`PHONE`; `NETWORK_IDENTIFIER` has `IP`/`PORT`/`DOMAIN`/`URL`. A `ClassificationClaim` can carry semantic type, subtype and sensitivity but **not** a domain or privacy/regulatory field; `Classification` gets influence `trust` from independently bound context and has no task-fidelity field. This is a description of today's foundation contract, not endorsement or authority to expand the enum in place. Keep accepted [sensitivity/trust separation](../decisions/002-sensitivity-and-trust-are-separate.md) and [semantic-judgment authority limits](../decisions/003-semantic-judgment-does-not-own-effects.md).
