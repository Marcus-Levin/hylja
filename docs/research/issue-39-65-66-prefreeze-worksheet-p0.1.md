## Decisions to write down before proposing a new oracle version

1. **#65 vocabulary and compatibility:** propose types/subtypes relevant to a possible v0 and explicit unstudied families, with definitions, ambiguity rules, mapping from current classes and a source-backed rationale; the protocol/evaluation owners later decide the benchmark's supported scope. Broad engineering content is not automatically an `ENGINEERING_IDENTIFIER`; an IP can have personal-data properties in context without becoming `PERSON`. New cases outside the reviewed scope are proposals, not secretly covered blind cases.
2. **#66 dimensions and provenance:** decide where privacy flags, sensitivity and *influence* trust live and how deterministic versus semantic disagreement is retained. An AI semantic judge may raise concern, not overrule rule-based secret evidence or authorize a send. Specify which task-fidelity predicates are attached per occurrence, per task or both, and how conflicting/high-cost demands become review/deny rather than a utility bypass. Treatment (`KEEP`/`MASK`/`BLOCK`, etc.) remains a separate destination-specific **policy** decision.
3. **Independent oracle representation:** agree source field identity, decoded-text Unicode code-point spans, normalization and candidate-input UTF-8 byte mapping, repeated/overlapping occurrences, subtype identity, negative controls and task-answer predicates. The current bounded D05 projector (a public JSON source-to-byte converter) handles only its documented ASCII/unescaped form; it is not a general blind-case mapper. A blank/unknown route or unsupported representation cannot become a positive safe-send observation. Do not put real customer, employee, infrastructure or credential examples—or blind contents, answers, seeds, keys, sensitive hashes or store locations—into Git, PRs, CI or chat; use clearly synthetic examples only.
4. **Role-specific approval:** after the revised v0-relevant research is independently reviewed, the protocol steward and evaluation owner decide the proposed #39 supported family/surface/representation/class matrix and exclusions under independent review. A separately designated policy reviewer decides treatments **for the actually observed test destination**, an engineering-task reviewer decides outcomes/fidelity, a scoring reviewer decides matching/denominators/critical gates, and independent blind author/reviewer/custodian and chronology reviewers check their own boundaries. Each approval cites its exact version, rationale, objections and date through the appropriate restricted route. The test owner records distinct decisions; that person cannot approve them all alone.
