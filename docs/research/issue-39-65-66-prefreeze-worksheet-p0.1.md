# Worksheet: what #65 and #66 must settle before a #39 synthetic v0 lock

**PUBLIC WORKSHEET — questions, not a taxonomy, accepted specification, human sign-off, benchmark lock or score.** Hylja is testing whether an engineering assistant can retain useful task information while private details are hidden before reaching an AI model. Only made-up local development examples exist. For actual implementation status, use [the plan](../plan.md#current-state); this worksheet cannot change it.

## Review roles — acceptance and independence are pending

Each of [#65 taxonomy research](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/65) and [#66 multidimensional design](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/66) needs a **human lead** and a **separate independent human reviewer**. This public worksheet records neither identities nor nominations. Before treating either study as reviewed, each person must accept the role and record relevant candidate-development, authorship and proposed [#39](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/39) roles through an approved private route. The reviewer must not be the author or director of the research being reviewed. An AI review cannot substitute for that check or sign-off. Publish only reviewed, privacy-safe conclusions.

For the private handoff, ask each prospective lead and reviewer to state: accepted role(s), expertise, authorship or candidate-development conflicts, any separate #39 role, objections, and the exact research version reviewed. Do not put names, contact details, real customer/employee/infrastructure/credential examples or hidden cases/answers in Git, PRs, issue comments or public chat. Assigning a role grants no taxonomy, task, policy or benchmark approval.

The broad #65 research still needs personal data (including Swedish personnummer/samordningsnummer), engineering domains (PLM/PDM, CAD, CAE, CAM, plant/asset, OT, IM and IT), standards and compatibility with the current [`classification.ts`](../../src/classification.ts). #66 must decide how semantic identity, privacy/regulatory attributes, sensitivity, evidence trust, task fidelity and policy treatment stay **separate**. Neither issue is resolved by a small D01/D02/D05 table. The research leads and their independent reviewers document versioned *v0-relevant findings*, limitations and remaining broad work; they do **not** approve #39's supported scope. Separately, the protocol steward and evaluation owner, under independent review, decide whether those findings are sufficient to propose a bounded partial #39 v0 and explicitly exclude unsupported families, surfaces, representations and classes. This worksheet grants neither research acceptance nor benchmark scope approval.

## Decisions to write down before proposing a new oracle version

1. **#65 vocabulary and compatibility:** propose types/subtypes relevant to a possible v0 and explicit unstudied families, with definitions, ambiguity rules, mapping from current classes and a source-backed rationale; the protocol/evaluation owners later decide the benchmark's supported scope. Broad engineering content is not automatically an `ENGINEERING_IDENTIFIER`; an IP can have personal-data properties in context without becoming `PERSON`. New cases outside the reviewed scope are proposals, not secretly covered blind cases.
2. **#66 dimensions and provenance:** decide where privacy flags, sensitivity and *influence* trust live and how deterministic versus semantic disagreement is retained. An AI semantic judge may raise concern, not overrule rule-based secret evidence or authorize a send. Specify which task-fidelity predicates are attached per occurrence, per task or both, and how conflicting/high-cost demands become review/deny rather than a utility bypass. Treatment (`KEEP`/`MASK`/`BLOCK`, etc.) remains a separate destination-specific **policy** decision.
3. **Independent oracle representation:** agree source field identity, decoded-text Unicode code-point spans, normalization and candidate-input UTF-8 byte mapping, repeated/overlapping occurrences, subtype identity, negative controls and task-answer predicates. The current bounded D05 projector (a public JSON source-to-byte converter) handles only its documented ASCII/unescaped form; it is not a general blind-case mapper. A blank/unknown route or unsupported representation cannot become a positive safe-send observation. Do not put real customer, employee, infrastructure or credential examples—or blind contents, answers, seeds, keys, sensitive hashes or store locations—into Git, PRs, CI or chat; use clearly synthetic examples only.
4. **Role-specific approval:** after the revised v0-relevant research is independently reviewed, the protocol steward and evaluation owner decide the proposed #39 supported family/surface/representation/class matrix and exclusions under independent review. A separately designated policy reviewer decides treatments **for the actually observed test destination**, an engineering-task reviewer decides outcomes/fidelity, a scoring reviewer decides matching/denominators/critical gates, and independent blind author/reviewer/custodian and chronology reviewers check their own boundaries. Each approval cites its exact version, rationale, objections and date through the appropriate restricted route. The test owner records distinct decisions; that person cannot approve them all alone.
