## Priority candidates

All four are **PRELIMINARY**. No criterion → named future owner → observable proof map is supplied here, so no requirement is claimed as covered: every acceptance checkbox, every adversarial requirement, every narrative security obligation and every checked mark stays verbatim at its live issue URL.

### P1 — [#13](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/13): **PRELIMINARY / NOT READY**

Ten unchecked acceptance items, the failure/negative section and the overlap section all remain. Item 1 requires **accepted** #68 semantics; decision 010 is proposed, so C1/C2 below are preparatory non-enforcing work and **not** #13 readiness.

- **C1 — typed failure path over trusted source locations.** Malformed or opaque parser status, stale or overlapping locations, mid-stream exception, invalid JSON/XML/**YAML** output, and an **unavailable generator** all return a typed restrictive failure: no partial success, no silent KEEP, no relaxed authorized action. No decision dependency.
- **C2 — structure-preserving reserialization with provenance.** Supported formats stay valid; transformation provenance reaches the privacy-safe evidence path. #114's provenance obligation lands here.
- **C3 — overlap and treatment semantics.** Compatible overlaps carry obligation-preserving composition and **fail only where no safe obligation-preserving rewrite can be established**, not on every overlap. Treatment selection stays policy-only (`decidePolicy`); secrets stay irreversible. C3 does not adopt the proposed semantics and does not start before decision 010 is accepted or narrowed.

Pointers: `docs/contracts/transformation-contract.md`, `docs/contracts/classification-unit-contract.md`, `src/structured-parsers.ts` (`rewriteFieldValues`), `src/policy.ts`.

### P3 — [#21](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/21): **PRELIMINARY / NOT READY**

Mock-transport children are preparatory; the real gateway integration keeps a **named owner** and a recorded human decision on endpoint and credentials. All seven checkboxes, the enforced-boundary narrative and the additional adversarial section stay on #21.

- **C1** destination profile + request/response translation (pure, conformance-tested).
- **C2** streaming holdback with split-secret, UTF-8/SSE-boundary and late-denial cases; its holdback composes with C3 before any release claim.

### P4 — [#22](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/22): **PRELIMINARY / NOT READY**

All five checkboxes and the full inbound-boundary/threat narrative stay, split by child.

- **A — inspect/hold/restrict, no restore.** The whole supported response including streamed text and tool-call deltas; fail closed on unknown payloads and incomplete high-risk streams; decisions correlate with [#20](https://github.com/Marcus-Levin/hylja/issues/20) privacy-safe evidence.
